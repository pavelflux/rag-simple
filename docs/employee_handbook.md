# Brightloop Employee Handbook

## Working Hours and Remote Work

Our core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core
hours you may arrange your day as you like, as long as your team knows how to
reach you.

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Manufacturing and
hardware-lab roles are on site full time because they need access to equipment.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from your manager and the COO.

## Paid Time Off

All full-time employees receive 28 days of paid vacation per year, in addition
to public holidays in their office's country. Vacation days accrue monthly.

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next calendar year.
Carried-over days expire on March 31 if they are not used.

Please request vacation in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for
absences longer than three days.

### Sick Leave

If you are sick, notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
Sick leave of up to three consecutive days does not require a doctor's note.
For longer absences, upload a doctor's note to the HR portal.

### Parental Leave

Brightloop offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all parents,
regardless of gender, on top of any statutory leave. It can be taken within the
first 18 months after a child's birth or adoption.

## Expenses and Travel

### What Can Be Expensed

Work-related costs are reimbursed when they are submitted with a receipt within
30 days. This includes travel to customer sites, conference tickets that your
manager approved, and books or online courses up to 500 euros per year.

### Travel Rules

- Book flights in economy class. Flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in
  premium economy.
- Hotel costs are capped at 150 euros per night (200 euros in London, Paris,
  New York, and San Francisco).
- The daily meal allowance during travel is 60 euros.
- Trains are preferred over flights for trips under 4 hours.

### Equipment

Every new employee chooses a laptop (MacBook Pro or Lenovo ThinkPad) and
receives a one-time home-office budget of 700 euros for a desk, chair, monitor,
or other equipment.

## Onboarding

During your first week you will meet your onboarding buddy, complete the
mandatory security training, and visit the Tallinn test warehouse (remote hires
visit within their first three months). By the end of your first 30 days you
should have shipped one small change or completed one customer visit.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in May and November. Salary
adjustments are decided once a year after the November review and take effect
on January 1.
