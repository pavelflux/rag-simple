LOOPY ROBOTS - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much weight can a Loopy L1 carry?

The Loopy L1 can lift and carry shelving units weighing up to 600 kg, including
the shelf itself. The Loopy L2 platform carries up to 400 kg, because some of
its capacity is used by the robotic arm. The L2 arm can pick individual items
weighing up to 3 kg.

How fast do the robots move?

Both models travel at up to 2 metres per second on open floor. In areas marked
as shared with people, Loopcloud automatically limits them to 1 metre per
second.

How long does the battery last?

A fully charged Loopy L1 runs for about 10 hours of normal operation. The L2
runs for about 8 hours because the arm uses extra power. Robots return to a
charging dock on their own when the battery drops below 20%. A full charge
takes about 90 minutes.

What does the warehouse floor need to look like?

The floor must be flat and sealed concrete or epoxy, with height differences of
no more than 5 mm per 2 metres. Robots navigate using small QR-code stickers
placed on the floor in a grid, roughly every 1.2 metres. No Wi-Fi dead zones
are allowed in the robot area.

How long does a typical deployment take?

A typical deployment of 50 robots takes 6 to 10 weeks from signed contract to
go-live. This includes a site survey, floor preparation, sticker installation,
software configuration, and two weeks of supervised trial runs.

What is the warranty?

Every Loopy robot comes with a 2-year standard warranty covering parts and
labour. Customers can buy an extended warranty that brings coverage to 5 years.
The warranty does not cover damage caused by forklifts, water, or operating on
floors that do not meet the floor requirements above.

How do I get support?

Customers with a Loopcloud subscription get 24/7 phone support and a target
response time of 1 hour for critical issues (a stopped fleet). Non-critical
issues are answered within 1 business day. Support can be reached at
support@brightloop.example or through the help button in Loopcloud.

Can Loopy robots work alongside forklifts?

Yes, but forklift lanes must be marked as restricted zones in Loopcloud. Robots
will never enter a restricted zone. We recommend physical barriers where
forklifts and robots cross frequently.

Is Loopcloud available on-premises?

Loopcloud normally runs in our cloud, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Customers
with strict data rules can buy an on-premises edition, which requires a
dedicated server in the warehouse and costs 30% more per robot per year.
