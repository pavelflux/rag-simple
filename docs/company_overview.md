# Brightloop Robotics — Company Overview

## Who We Are

Brightloop Robotics builds small autonomous robots that move inventory inside
warehouses. The company was founded in 2017 in Tallinn, Estonia, by Mara Velde
and Jonas Okafor, two former logistics engineers who were tired of watching
people walk twelve kilometres a day pushing carts between shelves.

Today Brightloop employs 214 people across three offices: Tallinn (headquarters
and engineering), Rotterdam (sales and customer success), and Austin, Texas
(North American operations). Around 60% of employees work in engineering or
manufacturing.

## Mission

Our mission is to "give every warehouse worker their afternoons back." We
believe automation should take over repetitive walking and lifting, so that
people can focus on the work that needs judgement: quality checks, exception
handling, and customer problems.

## Products

### Loopy L1

The Loopy L1 is our flagship robot. It is a low-profile mobile platform that
slides under a standard shelving unit, lifts it, and carries it to a packing
station. It was launched in 2019 and more than 4,000 units are in service today.

### Loopy L2

The Loopy L2, launched in March 2024, adds a robotic arm on top of the platform
so it can pick individual items rather than whole shelves. The L2 is sold mainly
to e-commerce customers with many small items.

### Loopcloud

Loopcloud is the software that coordinates a fleet of robots. It plans routes,
avoids traffic jams between robots, and shows warehouse managers a live map of
every robot. Loopcloud is sold as a yearly subscription priced per robot.

## Funding and Customers

Brightloop raised a 45 million euro Series B round in 2023, led by Northgate
Ventures. Major customers include grocery chains, fashion retailers, and
third-party logistics providers in eleven countries. The largest single
deployment is 380 robots in a fashion distribution centre near Lyon, France.

## Values

1. **Safety first.** No robot ships unless it passes our safety test suite.
2. **Boring reliability.** We prefer proven solutions to clever ones.
3. **Talk to the floor.** Engineers spend at least two days a quarter inside a
   customer warehouse.
